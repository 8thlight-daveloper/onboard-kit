# Questions to ask

Pick the ones that fit. Ask them with curiosity. Write the answers into the onboarding doc.

## The work

- What problem is this team here to solve?
- How will we know we succeeded?
- What is the most urgent thing right now? What is the most important?
- What has been tried before?

## The people

- Who should I meet first?
- Who knows the history?
- Who is affected by our work but not in our meetings?
- How do you prefer to communicate and receive feedback?

## The process

- How does work move from idea to done?
- Where does it get stuck?
- Who approves what?
- How do we handle urgent requests and surprises?

## The culture

- What does the team value?
- What should a newcomer avoid doing?
- How does the team celebrate and learn from mistakes?

## Expectations

- What do you expect from me in the first 30 days?
- What would make you say this engagement went well?
- How would you like to hear concerns?

## For the onboarding owner

- What would you tell your past self about starting here?
- What do people usually get wrong in their first month?
- What will you need from me when I leave?
