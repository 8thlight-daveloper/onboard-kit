# Variant: design work

Outline. Expand after the first engagement that uses it.

Adds the access, context, and norms specific to design work.

## Topics to cover

### Access to request

- Design tool workspace and the relevant files
- Design system documentation and component library
- Research repository and past findings
- Analytics and product usage data
- Prototyping and user testing tools
- Asset storage

### Learn the users

- Review existing research, personas, and journey maps
- Find out how the team talks to users and how often
- Ask for access to real users or the people closest to them
- Read support tickets or feedback channels

### Learn the product and system

- Walk the product as a user, end to end
- Learn the design system: what is settled, what is contested
- Note inconsistencies and ask about their history

### Learn the process

- How design work is requested, scoped, and prioritized
- How design is reviewed and who approves it
- How design hands off to development, and where it breaks down
- How the team measures design success

### Learn the constraints

- Brand, accessibility, and legal requirements
- Technical limits that shape what can ship
- Timelines and release cadence

### First contributions

- Start with a small improvement or critique you can finish in the first weeks
- Share work in progress early with the people who will build it
- Record design decisions and the reasons for them

## Open questions for this outline

- Which design-specific access tends to take longest to get?
- How does onboarding differ for research, interaction design, and visual design?
