# Variant: first consultants at the organization

Outline. Expand after the first engagement that uses it.

There is no shared history with outside consultants. The organization may not know how to work with us, and we may not know how to work with them. Your job includes building that relationship, drawing on the first 90 days approach.

## Topics to cover

### Establish the relationship

- Name an executive sponsor and an onboarding owner
- Agree on how the engagement is measured, in the client's terms
- Agree on how we communicate, how often, and with whom
- Decide who can say yes to access, spend, and scope changes

### Define the work

- Write down the problem in the client's words and confirm it
- Agree on what "done" means for the first 30 and 90 days
- Name what is out of scope

### Set the norms

- No team habits exist for you, so propose working agreements (`core/working-agreements.md`) and adopt them together
- Decide how work is tracked, reviewed, and shown
- Agree on where decisions are recorded

### Prepare the organization

- Tell the wider team who we are and why we are here
- Learn who is anxious about the engagement and listen to why
- Make early work visible so the engagement earns trust

### Plan the exit

- Decide who inherits each practice you introduce
- Write down what you set up and why

## Open questions for this outline

- What does a healthy sponsor relationship look like at week two and at week eight?
- How do we handle an organization with no onboarding material at all?
