# Arrange a working device

Confirm the approved equipment process, delivery expectations and setup support
with the responsible team. Keep shared setup instructions in the plan. Put a
person's equipment opportunity in an equipment arrangement linked to an actual
declared section instance; do not create a catalog-id binding automatically.
Do not put a home address, private shipping details or credentials in preparation.

Give the arrangement a stable unique id. Name a known accountable owner and a
nonsecret reference to the approved equipment request or handoff before marking
it arranged. Add known support contacts when available. Pending, requested or
deferred may retain missing details visibly, but supplied unknown references
are invalid. A targetDate is an optional real calendar planning date.

Confirm who resolves delayed delivery or failed setup and how the newcomer
can reach them before company access. Arranged means the onboarder has made
the minimum arrangement, not that a device arrived or works. The newcomer
verifies usability in the corresponding activity; preparation changes no record.
