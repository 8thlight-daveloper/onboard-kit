# Assign a pairing partner

Arrange a willing partner and suitable real ticket with a clear learning focus.
Keep shared pairing practices in the plan. Store the personal opportunity in
a pairing arrangement for an actual declared ticket-pairing section instance,
not in a shared answer or an invented pair-assign kind.

Use a stable unique id. Before claiming arranged, supply a known accountable
owner, at least one known helper contact and a nonsecret ticket or opportunity
reference. The owner is accountable for arranging; the helper is the partner.
Pending, requested and deferred may retain unassigned details visibly. A
supplied unknown helper is invalid, not a placeholder for a missing assignment.

Confirm availability, scope and a support route if the opportunity changes.
The optional real targetDate coordinates people and does not fail the newcomer.
Arranged means a minimum opportunity exists, not that pairing happened or a
ticket was delivered. Preparation edits change neither shared organization
answers nor the newcomer's outcomes or first-use state.
