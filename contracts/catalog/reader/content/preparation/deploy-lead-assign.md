# Assign an experienced deploy lead

Find an experienced person leading an appropriate deployment for the newcomer
to observe. Keep the deployment runbook and shared safety rules in the plan.
Use deploy-shadow for this personal arrangement and name an actual declared
shadowing section instance; the guide's title does not introduce a new kind.

An arranged item requires a known accountable owner, at least one known helper
and a nonsecret deployment opportunity reference. Here helpers are experienced
leads whom the newcomer observes. They are not automatically supporters for a
newcomer-led deployment. Keep a stable unique id and optional real planning
target. Missing details may be pending, requested or deferred, but unknown
supplied helpers or section references are invalid.

Confirm observation access and a route for questions without granting control
authority. Discuss verification, rollback and cancellation. A scheduled or
arranged deployment is not observed work or a verified newcomer outcome.
If conditions change, rearrange the opportunity; the date does not compel it.
