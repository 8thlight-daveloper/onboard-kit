# Arrange a merge opportunity

Ask the team to state whether this newcomer may merge their own PR, another
person's PR or only a defined subset. Keep that policy and mandatory reviews,
checks and separation of duties in the plan. Do not infer authorization from
source-host permissions or grant more access merely to complete onboarding.

Use pr-merge for an opportunity linked to an actual declared merge section
instance. Arranged requires a known accountable owner, at least one known
helper and a nonsecret PR or opportunity reference. Keep a stable unique id.
Pending, requested or deferred retain missing information visibly; unknown
supplied references are invalid. Targets are optional real planning dates.

Confirm that the selected opportunity is policy-permitted, that authorization
will be checked at action time and that support is available. Either author's
PR may serve only if team policy permits it. Arranged never means merged,
approved or verified. If a safe authorized opportunity is absent, keep the gap
instead of bypassing protection or manufacturing an outcome.
