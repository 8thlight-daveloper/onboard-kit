# Send meeting invitations

Coordinate introductions, regular meetings, retrospectives and check-ins that
are relevant to the selected work. Confirm participants, purpose, time-zone
interpretation and joining instructions without copying private calendar
contents. Keep shared meeting policies in the plan and individual invitations
in preparation; no automatic selection or meeting delivery is implied.

Use the meeting kind with stable unique arrangement ids and actual declared
section instances. Several arrangements may support one section when useful.
Arranged requires a known owner and nonsecret meeting reference; helpers are
optional for this kind. Pending, requested or deferred retain missing details
visibly. Unknown supplied section or contact references remain invalid.

Check that joining guidance is usable with the access expected at that time,
and identify who handles missing invitations. Targets are planning goals, not
attendance failures. An invitation sent or an arranged meeting is not proof
of joining, attendance, understanding or a completed conversation. Those
outcomes are separate from onboarder preparation.
