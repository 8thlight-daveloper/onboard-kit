# Arrange office access and badge

Coordinate the approved badge or visitor process, arrival instructions and a
reachable business host before the first on-site visit. Shared security rules
and arrival routes belong in the plan. Keep a person's arrangement in the
office kind, linked to an actual declared office-access section instance.
Do not collect a home address, private itinerary or access code in preparation.

Use a stable unique id. Before marking arranged, provide a known accountable
owner and a nonsecret reference to the office request or approved instructions.
Add known host contacts when assigned. Pending, requested or deferred can
retain missing information visibly; unknown supplied contacts or sections are
invalid. A targetDate supports coordination rather than enforcing attendance.

Confirm a fallback if the badge or host is unavailable, including a route that
works before entering. Arranged is not proof that the badge or entry works.
The newcomer verifies authorized access; neither urgency nor onboarding
completion permits borrowed badges or bypassing physical security controls.
