# Identify a first owned ticket

Agree a suitably scoped real task, expected result and support contact. Avoid
selecting a task solely to satisfy an onboarding counter. Keep the shared
delivery process in the plan and the personal opportunity in a ticket
arrangement linked to an actual declared ticket-ownership section instance.

An arranged ticket item requires a known accountable owner, at least one known
helper and a nonsecret task reference. Give it a stable unique id. Pending,
requested and deferred can show missing details; unknown supplied contacts
or sections are invalid. An optional real targetDate is a planning aid, not
a deadline that authorizes work or fails someone.

Discuss permission boundaries, dependencies and how to request scope changes.
If no suitable task exists, retain the gap and arrange support rather than
inventing a ticket. Assigned or arranged does not mean accepted, delivered or
verified by the newcomer. Do not update the personal record or populate
answers through guessed recipient-to-template bindings.
