# Assign onboarding support

Identify the manager, onboarding owner, buddy and initial help contacts as
applicable. Recipient manager, onboardingOwner and buddy reference known contacts
in the plan directory; do not invent names or assume the first directory entry.
Keep shared support routes and policies in the plan and individual assignments
in this person's preparation. Explain any missing assignment visibly.

When a support arrangement is useful, use the existing support kind and an
actual declared section instance. Do not manufacture an instance or add a
support-assign arrangement kind. The accountable owner and assigned helpers
are distinct roles, even when the same known contact fills both.

Pending, requested and deferred describe preparation, not newcomer outcomes.
An arranged support item requires an owner; its reference is optional under
the current support rules. Unknown contacts are invalid even while pending.
Confirm a reachable initial route and fallback with the people involved.
An assignment or arranged status is not proof that help is available, access
works or the newcomer has met anyone. Dates are support planning goals only.
