# Arrange a PR review opportunity

Identify a suitable real change and an experienced discussion contact. Match
the opportunity to the newcomer's context and authority. Shared review rules
stay in the plan; the person-specific opportunity uses pr-review and an actual
declared review section instance. Catalog selection alone creates no instance.

Before marking arranged, supply a known owner, at least one known helper and
a nonsecret PR reference. Keep a stable unique arrangement id. Missing owner,
opportunity or helper remains visible while pending, requested or deferred;
unknown supplied references remain invalid. A targetDate is optional and a
planning goal. Do not include restricted patch contents or credentials in notes.

Confirm who can answer questions and whether the newcomer can approve or only
comment. Do not grant extra approval authority for onboarding. Arranged is
not a substantive review, an approval or a newcomer outcome. Preserve honest
gaps if the PR changes, closes or becomes unsuitable before the activity.
