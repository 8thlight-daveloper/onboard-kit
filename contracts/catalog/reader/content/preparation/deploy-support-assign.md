# Assign deployment support

Arrange a suitable authorized deployment and experienced support for the
newcomer to lead it. Keep the runbook, readiness and stop rules in the plan.
Use deploy-lead for the person-specific arrangement and an actual declared
lead-deployment section instance. Never infer readiness from a planning date.

An arranged item needs a known accountable owner, at least one known helper
and a nonsecret opportunity reference. For this kind helpers are experienced
observers or supporters, not the lead being shadowed. Give the item a stable
unique id and optional real targetDate. Pending, requested or deferred keep
missing support visible; unknown supplied contacts and sections are invalid.

Confirm support availability, authority to act, safe scope, verification and
rollback decisions. Do not grant production rights solely for onboarding.
Arranged is a minimum preparation claim, not permission at action time,
successful deployment or a verified newcomer outcome. Preserve the committed
ownership boundaries: no shared-answer injection or personal-record writes.
