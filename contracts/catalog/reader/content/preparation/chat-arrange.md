# Arrange chat membership

Confirm the organization's actual chat service, intended account and relevant
workspaces or channels. Keep shared joining instructions and fallback routes
in the plan. Do not assume a provider or create an account automatically.
Identify an initial route that does not depend on already having chat access.

Use access arrangements for person-specific requests against actual declared
chat section instances. Distinct resources may need separate arrangements with
stable unique ids. Arranged requires a known owner and nonsecret reference;
helpers are included only when assigned. Pending, requested and deferred may
retain visible gaps, but unknown supplied contacts or sections are invalid.
Do not store invitations containing secret tokens or recovery material.

Confirm who can resolve expired invitations or missing channel membership.
Targets coordinate support and do not fail the newcomer. A sent invitation or
arranged request does not establish that messages can be exchanged; the
newcomer verifies the actual capability. No chat-specific kind or outcome field
is introduced, and neither plan answers nor personal record are changed.
