# Days 30 to 90

Goal: move from learning to contributing steadily, and leave the team better able to continue without you.

## Build momentum

- [ ] Take on work with real scope, agreed with your onboarding owner
- [ ] Share small improvements as you find them, with the reasoning behind each
- [ ] Pair or co-work with teammates so knowledge moves in both directions

## Diagnose the situation

Name which of these describes the engagement. The answer changes what you do next.

- Start-up: building something new
- Turnaround: fixing something in trouble
- Realignment: a team or product that lost its way
- Sustaining success: protecting something that works

Check your read with your onboarding owner. Misreading the situation is a common early mistake.

## Build relationships beyond the team

- [ ] Meet the people your team depends on and the people who depend on it
- [ ] Learn what they need from the team and where handoffs fail

## Plan for your exit from day one

- [ ] List what only you know and write it down
- [ ] Teach one teammate each skill or system you introduce
- [ ] Keep the onboarding doc current and name its owner

## Check in at day 90

- [ ] Compare results to the success measures agreed before day one
- [ ] Decide with the client what happens next: continue, change shape, or wind down
- [ ] Hand the onboarding doc to its client owner
