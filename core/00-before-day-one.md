# Before day one

Most delays in the first week come from access that was not ready. Start these early.

## Confirm with the onboarding owner

- [ ] Start date, working hours, and time zone overlap with the team
- [ ] Who you report to day to day, and who owns the engagement on the client side
- [ ] What success looks like at 30 and 90 days, in the client's words
- [ ] Where the team talks (chat, email lists, standups) and which channels to join
- [ ] Whether you are working on client hardware or your own

## Start access requests

Use `access-checklist.md`. Request everything on day minus five if you can. Approvals often take days.

## Read ahead

- [ ] Any product, strategy, or architecture overview the client can share
- [ ] The team's recent meeting notes or decision log, if one exists
- [ ] Public material about the organization and its users

## Prepare your own plan

- [ ] Draft the questions you want answered in week one (`templates/questions-to-ask.md`)
- [ ] List the people you want to meet in the first two weeks
- [ ] Block time on your calendar for reading and listening before you commit to work
