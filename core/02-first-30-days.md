# First 30 days

Goal: understand the work, the people, and the expectations well enough to deliver something that matters. Borrowing from the first 90 days idea: spend this stretch learning, and secure one early, visible win.

## Learn

- [ ] Map how work moves from idea to done, and where it stalls
- [ ] Learn the history behind the current state. Ask why things are the way they are before proposing change
- [ ] Find out who decides what, and how decisions get made
- [ ] Learn what the team is proud of and what they avoid talking about
- [ ] Complete `people-map.md` for your closest ten working relationships

## Align

- [ ] Confirm expectations with your onboarding owner, in writing
- [ ] Agree on how you will report progress and how often
- [ ] Review `working-agreements.md` with the team and adopt what fits

## Deliver

- [ ] Pick one early win: small, visible, and valued by the team
- [ ] Finish it, and show the team the result
- [ ] Share what you observed so far as questions, not verdicts

## Check in at day 30

- [ ] Review progress against the goals set in week one
- [ ] Ask your onboarding owner and two teammates for candid feedback
- [ ] Revise the 90 day plan (`03-days-30-to-90.md`) with what you now know
