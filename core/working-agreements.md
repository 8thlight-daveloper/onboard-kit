# Working agreements

Agree on how you will work together. Write down what the team already does, then add what is missing. Keep the list short enough to remember.

## Communication

- Where do questions go: chat channel, ticket comment, or direct message?
- How fast do people expect a reply?
- When do we call instead of write?
- How do we share status with people outside the team?

## Meetings

- Which meetings are required for you?
- Who runs them, and where are the notes kept?
- How does the team handle time zones and working hours?

## Decisions

- Who decides what? Who must be consulted?
- Where do we record decisions so later readers find them?

## Feedback

- How do we give feedback on work? How do we receive it?
- How do we raise a concern early?

## Availability

- Core overlap hours: `<hours and time zone>`
- How we signal time off or focus time: `<practice>`
