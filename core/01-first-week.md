# First week

Goal: be able to work with the team without asking for help on basics. Learn before you advise.

## Day one

- [ ] Meet your onboarding owner and confirm the plan for the week
- [ ] Check every access item. Escalate gaps the same day
- [ ] Meet the people you will work with most
- [ ] Ask for one small, real task you can finish this week

## Days two to four

- [ ] Attend the team's regular meetings and listen
- [ ] Work through the setup steps for your discipline (see the variant)
- [ ] Hold a short one-on-one with each teammate: what they own, what hurts, what they would change
- [ ] Write down the team's terms, acronyms, and names for things
- [ ] Fix every gap you find in the setup docs as you go

## End of week

- [ ] Finish the small task, or know exactly what blocks it
- [ ] Share what you learned with the onboarding owner and ask what you misread
- [ ] Update the onboarding doc with everything that was wrong or missing
- [ ] Agree on goals for the first 30 days
