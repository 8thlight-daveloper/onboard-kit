# How to build your onboarding doc

You are joining an organization and want a doc that gets you, and the people after you, productive quickly. This guide turns the core and variant files here into that doc.

## Who it is for

The doc has two readers. You use it now. The client keeps using it after the engagement ends, for new hires and for the next consultant. Write it so someone who never met you can follow it.

## Steps

1. **Choose a team-state variant.**
   - Joining people who already work together: `variants/team-state/established-team.md`
   - Arriving as the first consultants: `variants/team-state/first-consultants.md`
2. **Choose a discipline variant.**
   - `variants/discipline/software-dev.md`
   - `variants/discipline/design.md`
3. **Start a blank doc outside this repo.** Put the owner and the last review date at the top. Add a heading for each part: welcome, your first week, who to meet, access, setup for your discipline, how we work, your first 30 and 90 days, terms we use, and where to get help.
4. **Pull in the matching sections** from `core/` and your two variants. Delete what does not apply. Keep what does.
5. **Fill every placeholder** in angle brackets. If you cannot fill one yet, write the question you need answered and who can answer it.
6. **Ask the onboarding owner to review.** They know what is stale.
7. **Hand it over** (see below).

## Keep it alive while you work

- Fix the doc the day you find it wrong. The first week shows you what is missing.
- Note each question you had to ask. A question asked twice belongs in the doc.
- Date each section's last check.

## Hand it over

Before you leave:

- Move the doc into the client's own system, in a place their team already looks.
- Name an owner at the client who will keep it current.
- Remove anything that only made sense with you in the room.
- Confirm no credentials or secrets are in it. Link to the system that holds them.

## What not to include

Keep secrets out of the doc itself. Point to where access is granted and who grants it. The doc says how to get access, not the access.
